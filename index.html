How fingerability is checked

Every voicing contains all required chord tones, permits repeated notes, and has a modeled assignment to at most four fingers. A barre may cross a higher fretted note, but never an open, unplayed, or lower-fretted string. Conservative mode limits fret span according to physical spacing. These are candidate grips, not a guarantee of comfort: finger shape, wrist angle and guitar neck vary. CAGED tags identify exact transpositions of explicitly listed templates, not every possible CAGED-derived voicing.
